# What the Registrar Does

House of Stake’s role in the NEAR Top-Level Account namespace was established through a **NEAR Tokenholder vote under HSP-028**, which was ratified on **September 4, 2026 at 11:00 PM UTC**.

Through this decision, **House of Stake became the governance authority for the NEAR Top-Level Account namespace root**, with the **[House of Stake Security Council](/docs/structure/security-council) acting as the Registrar**.

The Registrar’s role is mainly operational — to securely execute valid Top-Level Account (“TLA”) creation requests and protect the integrity of the namespace root.

## Responsibilities

The Registrar is responsible for:

- **Creating Top-Level Accounts:** Execute valid requests to create new bare TLAs on NEAR.
- **Verifying requests:** Confirm that creation requests meet the required governance, technical, and security conditions before execution.
- **Secure execution:** Carry out Registrar Actions through the approved multisig or threshold-signing process.
- **Protective actions:** Delay or suspend execution when there is a security issue, unauthorized request, vulnerability, or other serious risk.
- **Transparency:** Ensure Registrar Actions can be publicly reviewed and verified on-chain.

## What the Registrar Does Not Do

The Registrar does not decide:

- who receives a namespace;
- pricing or fees;
- marketplace or leasing rules;
- customer policies;
- account recovery; or
- commercial decisions.

These responsibilities belong to the **nearaccounts.com team**.

The Registrar also does not control TLAs after they are created. House of Stake and the Security Council cannot access users’ accounts, keys, or assets.

## Governance and Accountability

The Registrar operates under the authority of **NEAR Tokenholders through House of Stake** and must follow the applicable House of Stake governance and security requirements set out in the [Registrar Charter](/docs/structure/legal-documents/tla-registrar-charter) and the [Constitutional Documents](/docs/structure/legal-documents/constitution).

Its authority is intentionally limited and cannot be expanded through operational decisions alone.

## In Simple Terms

- **House of Stake governs the namespace root.**
- **The Security Council operates the Registrar.**
- **nearaccounts.com manages the product and commercial side.**
- **Users remain in control of their accounts and assets.**
